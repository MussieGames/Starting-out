01 — Executive Summary

A keepsake system for childhood — not another photo dump.

Flourish helps parents capture, organise, and preserve their child’s earliest moments in one beautiful, secure place — with optional printed hardcover books. Parents today face camera-roll chaos and growing unease about posting children’s faces on social media or feeding them into AI-trained photo clouds. Generic storage apps store. Social apps share. Neither treasures.

Business model: Recurring subscriptions (Bloom and premium tiers) plus high-margin printed keepsakes. No ads. No data sales. No AI training on children’s photos.

The parenting apps category is growing at roughly 12% CAGR toward multi-billion-dollar scale. Peers such as Tinybeans have validated private family memory platforms via subscription + photobook commerce — proving demand while leaving room for a scrapbook-native, privacy-absolute brand.

03 — The Problem

Childhood vanishes into scattered digital clutter.

First smiles, first steps, late-night feeds, and ordinary “in-between” moments scatter across camera rolls, chat threads, and half-finished albums. Years later, parents remember the big events — and mourn the everyday love they failed to keep in one place.

This is not a storage problem. It is a regret problem.
PainReality today
FragmentationPhotos, videos, notes, and milestones live in different apps
Social riskPublic platforms expose children to advertisers, strangers, and scrapers
AI & data anxietyCloud photo products increasingly train models on personal media
Missed firstsParents often learn about milestones after they happened
Digital decayPhones break, accounts lock, formats change — nothing tactile remains

Category leaders exist because parents already reject public social media for family moments. Flourish’s insight: parents don’t want another photo dump — they want a keepsake system.

05 — Market Opportunity

A growing category with proven willingness to pay.

Global parenting apps are estimated at approximately USD $1.7–1.9B (2025–2026), with forecasts toward USD $3–6B by 2030–2035 at ~12% CAGR.

Beachhead

Australia first, then English-speaking markets (NZ, UK, US, Canada). ~300,000+ births per year in Australia; dual-income, privacy-conscious millennial and Gen Z parents; strong fit for a premium, trust-led consumer brand.

Why now

  • Gen Z / younger millennial parents prefer private, intentional sharing over public feeds
  • Rising concern about children’s images training commercial AI
  • Validated economics: peers scale via subscription + photobooks (~95k paid subscribers at Tinybeans group scale post-Qeepsake)
  • Fragmented competition leaves room for a scrapbook-native, privacy-absolute brand
