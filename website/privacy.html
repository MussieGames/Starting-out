This Privacy Policy explains how Flourish collects, holds, uses and discloses personal information, and how you can access or correct it, or make a complaint.

Flourish is operated by an Australian sole trader (not a company). References to “Flourish”, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the sole trader trading as Flourish.

1. Our approach to privacy

Flourish is built around a simple promise: childhood memories are intimate, and they should stay that way.

We design our practices to align with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), as a matter of best practice and brand commitment — including while Flourish may qualify as a small business operator under the Act.

If the Privacy Act applies to us for any reason (for example, if turnover exceeds the small-business threshold, an exception applies, or we opt in), this Policy is intended to meet APP 1 requirements for an APP privacy policy.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use children’s photos or family content to train artificial intelligence models. We do not serve advertising based on your family’s content.

2. Who this Policy covers

This Policy applies to:

  • visitors to https://www.goflourish.com.au
  • people who join our waitlist or contact us
  • parents, guardians and family members who use Flourish products or services when they become available
  • personal information about children that is uploaded or created by a parent/guardian account holder

Children: Flourish is for parents and guardians. Children are not intended to create accounts on this website. When the app launches, a parent or guardian will control the child’s story and any personal information about the child.

3. What personal information we collect

3.1 Website and waitlist (current)

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact details — name (if provided), email address
  • Waitlist details — email, signup source/page, approximate time of signup, basic device/browser information (user agent)
  • Communications — messages you send us (e.g. email to hello@goflourish.com.au)
  • Technical data — IP address, device/browser type, pages viewed, referring URL, and similar analytics/security logs (if enabled)

3.2 Flourish product (when launched)

When you use the Flourish app or related services, we may also collect:

  • Account details — name, email, password or authentication identifiers, profile preferences
  • Child profile details you choose to provide — child’s first name or nickname, date of birth or age, and related story settings
  • Content you upload or create — photos, videos, voice memos, captions, journal entries, scrapbook layouts, stickers/tags, and milestone/“firsts” records
  • Sharing settings — who you invite (e.g. grandparents), their contact details, and access permissions you set
  • Purchase and billing data (if you buy a subscription or printed book) — payment token/status via our payment provider (we aim not to store full card numbers ourselves), billing email, order details, shipping address for print products
  • Support data — information you provide when contacting support

3.3 Sensitive information

Under the Privacy Act, “sensitive information” includes information about health, and certain other categories. Milestone or developmental information, and photos/videos of children, can be highly personal.

We only collect sensitive information where it is reasonably necessary for Flourish, and where required we will obtain consent (usually from the parent/guardian account holder) or otherwise handle it as permitted by law.

3.4 What we do not intentionally collect

We do not intentionally collect tax file numbers, government identifiers for marketing, or biometric templates for facial recognition advertising.

4. How we collect personal information

We collect personal information:

  • directly from you — waitlist forms, account signup, in-app entry, email, support
  • automatically — when you use our website or app (logs, security, basic analytics)
  • from people you invite — if a family member accepts a share invitation you send
  • from service providers — e.g. payment confirmation from a payment processor

Where reasonable and practicable, we collect personal information only from you (or, for a child, from the parent/guardian who controls the account).

If you do not provide information we need (for example, an email for the waitlist), we may not be able to provide the relevant service.

5. Why we collect, hold, use and disclose personal information

We collect, hold, use and disclose personal information for purposes including:

  • operating the website and waitlist, and sending waitlist / launch updates you asked for
  • creating and managing accounts and providing Flourish features (capture, scrapbook, firsts, journal, private sharing, print)
  • securing our systems, preventing abuse, and troubleshooting
  • processing payments and fulfilling print orders (when available)
  • responding to enquiries and complaints
  • improving Flourish in aggregated or de-identified ways where practicable
  • complying with Australian law and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims
  • sending service messages (e.g. security, billing, material changes to terms)

Direct marketing

We may send you marketing or product updates by email where you have consented (for example, by joining the waitlist) or where otherwise permitted by the Spam Act 2003 (Cth) and the Privacy Act.

You can opt out at any time using the unsubscribe link in an email or by contacting hello@goflourish.com.au. We will not sell your email list.

Secondary purposes

We only use or disclose personal information for another purpose if you consent, you would reasonably expect it and it is related (or, for sensitive information, directly related) to the primary purpose, or the law requires or authorises it.

6. Children’s personal information

Flourish treats children’s personal information with heightened care.

  • A parent or legal guardian is the account holder and decision-maker.
  • We collect children’s information to help that parent preserve and organise their child’s story — not to build advertising profiles.
  • We do not use children’s content to train AI models.
  • Shared access (e.g. grandparents) is controlled by the parent/guardian’s permissions.
  • Parents/guardians can request access, correction or deletion of their account and associated child content (subject to legal retention needs).

We will continue to monitor the OAIC’s Children’s Online Privacy Code developments and update our practices and notices as required.

7. Disclosure of personal information

We may disclose personal information to:

  • service providers who help us operate Flourish (hosting, databases, email delivery, analytics, payment processing, print fulfilment, customer support tools), under contracts that require them to protect the information
  • people you choose — family members you invite to a private album or share
  • professional advisers (e.g. lawyers, accountants) where reasonably necessary
  • regulators or law enforcement where required or authorised by law
  • a buyer or successor if we sell or transfer the business (we will take reasonable steps to require ongoing privacy protection)

We do not disclose personal information to advertisers for targeted advertising of children’s content.

8. Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers may store or process personal information outside Australia.

As at the date of this Policy, overseas disclosure is likely, including to recipients in:

  • the United States (for example, transactional email and certain cloud infrastructure providers), and
  • other countries where our providers operate support or infrastructure (which can change over time)

Where practicable we prefer Australian regions (for example, Google Cloud / Firebase australia-southeast1). Even then, a provider’s global support, backup, or subprocessors may involve overseas processing.

When we disclose personal information to an overseas recipient, we take steps required by APP 8 (where it applies), including taking reasonable steps to ensure the recipient does not breach the APPs in relation to the information, unless an exception applies (such as your consent, or where required/authorised by law).

9. How we hold and secure personal information

We hold personal information in electronic systems (and occasionally in email or documents needed to run the business).

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Measures may include:

  • access controls and authentication
  • encryption in transit and at rest where supported by our providers
  • least-privilege access for operators and contractors
  • monitoring and logging for security events
  • staff/contractor confidentiality expectations

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe your account or information has been compromised, contact us immediately.

Data breaches

If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm, and the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme applies to us, we will assess and notify affected individuals and the OAIC as required by law. As best practice, we aim to notify affected people of serious breaches even where notification is not strictly mandated.

10. How long we keep personal information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes set out in this Policy (including providing the service you asked for), and as required or permitted by law (for example, tax or dispute records).

  • Waitlist emails: kept until you unsubscribe/ask us to delete, or until we close the waitlist and no longer need the list for launch communications (unless you become a customer and the information moves into an account record).
  • Account and content: kept while your account is active. After deletion requests, we will delete or de-identify within a reasonable period, except where we must retain information for legal reasons or security backups that rotate out on a schedule.

11. Access and correction

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading.

Email hello@goflourish.com.au with the subject line “Privacy access request” or “Privacy correction request”.

We will respond within a reasonable time. If we refuse a request (where permitted), we will tell you why (unless unreasonable to do so) and how to complain.

Parents/guardians may make requests in relation to personal information about their child that is held in their Flourish account.

12. Anonymity and pseudonyms

Where it is lawful and practicable, you may deal with us anonymously or using a pseudonym (for example, general website browsing). For the waitlist, account creation, payments and shared albums, we usually need identifiable details to provide the service.

13. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies for essential site function, security, and (if enabled) analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect site features.

14. Third-party links

Our website may link to third-party sites. Their privacy practices are their own. We encourage you to read their policies.

15. Complaints

If you have a privacy concern or complaint:

  1. Contact us first at hello@goflourish.com.au (subject: “Privacy complaint”).
  2. We will acknowledge and investigate in good faith and aim to respond within 30 days (or explain if more time is needed).

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC):

  • Website: https://www.oaic.gov.au
  • Phone: 1300 363 992
  • Online complaint form via the OAIC website

If you are overseas, you may also have rights under local laws (for example, GDPR if you are in the EEA/UK). Contact us and we will help where we can.

16. Overseas visitors (brief note)

Flourish is operated from Australia and primarily intended for Australian families. If you access Flourish from outside Australia, your information may be processed in Australia and in other countries as described in section 8.

If you are in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom or similar jurisdictions, additional rights may apply (access, erasure, restriction, portability, objection). Contact hello@goflourish.com.au to exercise applicable rights. Our legal basis for processing will typically be consent and/or steps needed to provide a service you request.

If you are in California or another US state with consumer privacy laws, you may have rights to know, delete, or opt out of certain sales/sharing. Flourish does not sell personal information. Contact us to make a request.

17. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change, and the current version will be published at https://www.goflourish.com.au/privacy.html. If changes are material, we may also notify you by email (for waitlist or account holders) where appropriate.
