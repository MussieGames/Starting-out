## 1. Our approach to privacy

Flourish is built around a simple promise: childhood memories are intimate, and they should stay that way.

We design our practices to align with the **Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)** in the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth), as a matter of best practice and brand commitment — including while Flourish may qualify as a small business operator under the Act.

If the Privacy Act applies to us for any reason (for example, if turnover exceeds the small-business threshold, an exception applies, or we opt in), this Policy is intended to meet **APP 1** requirements for an APP privacy policy.

We do **not** sell personal information. We do **not** use children’s photos or family content to train artificial intelligence models. We do **not** serve advertising based on your family’s content.

## 3. What personal information we collect

### 3.1 Website and waitlist (current)

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

- **Identity and contact details** — name (if provided), email address
- **Waitlist details** — email, signup source/page, approximate time of signup, basic device/browser information (user agent)
- **Communications** — messages you send us (e.g. email to hello@goflourish.com.au)
- **Technical data** — IP address, device/browser type, pages viewed, referring URL, and similar analytics/security logs (if enabled)

### 3.2 Flourish product (when launched)

When you use the Flourish app or related services, we may also collect:

- **Account details** — name, email, password or authentication identifiers, profile preferences
- **Child profile details you choose to provide** — child’s first name or nickname, date of birth or age, and related story settings
- **Content you upload or create** — photos, videos, voice memos, captions, journal entries, scrapbook layouts, stickers/tags, and milestone/“firsts” records
- **Sharing settings** — who you invite (e.g. grandparents), their contact details, and access permissions you set
- **Purchase and billing data** (if you buy a subscription or printed book) — payment token/status via our payment provider (we aim not to store full card numbers ourselves), billing email, order details, shipping address for print products
- **Support data** — information you provide when contacting support

### 3.3 Sensitive information

Under the Privacy Act, “sensitive information” includes information about health, and certain other categories. Milestone or developmental information, and photos/videos of children, can be highly personal.

We only collect sensitive information where it is reasonably necessary for Flourish, and where required we will obtain **consent** (usually from the parent/guardian account holder) or otherwise handle it as permitted by law.

### 3.4 What we do not intentionally collect

We do not intentionally collect tax file numbers, government identifiers for marketing, or biometric templates for facial recognition advertising.

## 4. How we collect personal information

We collect personal information:

- **directly from you** — waitlist forms, account signup, in-app entry, email, support
- **automatically** — when you use our website or app (logs, security, basic analytics)
- **from people you invite** — if a family member accepts a share invitation you send

## 5. Why we collect, hold, use and disclose personal information

We collect, hold, use and disclose personal information for purposes including:

- operating the website and waitlist, and sending waitlist / launch updates you asked for

- sending service messages (e.g. security, billing, material changes to terms)

### Direct marketing

We may send you marketing or product updates by email where you have consented (for example, by joining the waitlist) or where otherwise permitted by the *Spam Act 2003* (Cth) and the Privacy Act.

You can opt out at any time using the unsubscribe link in an email or by contacting hello@goflourish.com.au. We will not sell your email list.

### Secondary purposes

We only use or disclose personal information for another purpose if you consent, you would reasonably expect it and it is related (or, for sensitive information, directly related) to the primary purpose, or the law requires or authorises it.

Flourish treats children’s personal information with heightened care.

## 9. How we hold and secure personal information

We hold personal information in electronic systems (and occasionally in email or documents needed to run the business).

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Measures may include:

- access controls and authentication
- encryption in transit and at rest where supported by our providers
- least-privilege access for operators and contractors
- monitoring and logging for security events
- staff/contractor confidentiality expectations

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe your account or information has been compromised, contact us immediately.

### Data breaches

If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm, and the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme applies to us, we will assess and notify affected individuals and the OAIC as required by law. As best practice, we aim to notify affected people of serious breaches even where notification is not strictly mandated.
